{{ if gt .Trends.WeightKG 0.0 }}{{ end }}

The best power each month at five durations, against your all-time best (dashed). A month without a hard effort at a duration shows nothing rather than a low number.{{ if gt .Trends.WeightKG 0.0 }} W/kg uses today's weight for every month: fitbase keeps no weight history.{{ end }}

efficiency factor steady rides

Normalized power ÷ average HR on steady rides (IF 0.55–0.80, 45 minutes or more), outdoors and indoors apart: indoors runs hotter. Rising EF at the same intensity is aerobic fitness.

heart rate and power at matched effort

Steady rides near one power, or one heart rate. Lower heart rate at the same power, or more power at the same heart rate, is aerobic fitness; heat, fatigue and illness push the other way.

durability Pw:HR decoupling, long steady rides

How far heart rate drifts against power from the first half of a steady ride of 90 minutes or more to the second. Under 5% (dashed) is aerobically durable.

cadence average, cycling

intensity rides by IF

How hard each ride was overall: a season of mostly 0.75–0.85 rides is a lot of tempo.

intensity split time in power zones

A polarized plan keeps roughly 80% of the time easy and most of the rest hard; a large middle share means a lot of tempo and threshold riding.

consistency days active

effort how hard it felt against TSS

Rides you rated on the ride page. The same TSS feeling harder lately can mean fatigue; feeling easier, fitness.
