{{/* ── Aerobic durability ──────────────────────────────────────────────── */}}

aerobic durability weekly

efficiency factor decoupling %

Efficiency factor is normalized power ÷ average HR on endurance rides (IF 0.55–0.80, 45 minutes or more): rising EF at the same intensity is aerobic fitness. Decoupling is how far HR drifts against power over steady rides of 90 minutes or more: under 5% is durable.
